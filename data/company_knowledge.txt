=== SECTION: About TechXfluence ===
SOURCE: https://www.techxfluence.com/
TechXfluence (written "Techxfluence" on the website) is a technology community and event ecosystem focused on building India's tech community. Its tagline is "Where Technology Meets Influence." The community tagline is "Built by the community - We Connect". The community slogan is "Connect. Learn. Build. Influence."
TechXfluence is based in Chennai, Tamil Nadu, India.
It brings together developers, entrepreneurs, startups, students, creators and technology enthusiasts through events, networking and collaborative learning. It runs in-person meetups, workshops, hackathons and conferences across India, and aims to keep the community active between events.

=== SECTION: Mission, Vision and Values ===
SOURCE: https://www.techxfluence.com/about
Mission: "To create a thriving technology ecosystem where people connect, learn, build and grow together."
Vision: "To become India's most influential technology community platform." This means a community of innovators building the future.
Values:
- Community first: the people involved come first; a sense of belonging matters more than vanity metrics.
- Learn by building: hands-on hackathons, workshops and demos are preferred over passive talks.
- Open and inclusive: the community welcomes people at every career stage, in every city and from every background.
- Give before you take: mentorship, introductions and knowledge are shared freely; contribution is the main currency.

=== SECTION: Membership Plans ===
SOURCE: https://www.techxfluence.com/membership
There are three membership tiers:
- Free Member: Rs. 0 forever. Includes event attendance, ticket and QR check-in history, a private group for each event you attend, and the community feed plus monthly newsletter. The page says "Come to anything. Always."
- Pro Member: Rs. 499 per month (labelled "Most popular"). Everything in Free, plus seeing who is attending events, the full member directory, priority registration on limited seats, and member pricing on paid events (25% off according to the homepage comparison table).
- Elite Member: Rs. 1,499 per month. Everything in Pro, plus speaking slots and session hosting, 1:1 mentorship matching, chapter leadership opportunities and a featured directory profile. Member pricing on paid events is 50% off according to the homepage comparison table.
Volunteer and Community Ambassador roles are open to members of any tier.
Note: the homepage comparison table marks priority registration, speaking slots, 1:1 mentorship matching and chapter leadership as "Coming soon", so availability of those perks may vary - check the membership page.

=== SECTION: Membership Benefits and Policy ===
SOURCE: https://www.techxfluence.com/membership
Benefits described across tiers: free or discounted access to select events and member pricing on others; priority registration for limited-seat workshops and hackathons; exclusive events such as member-only sessions and founder dinners; networking introductions to founders, engineers, designers and investors; partner discounts on developer tools, cloud credits and courses; leadership opportunities such as hosting tracks and serving as an ambassador; badges, points and certificates for community contributions.
Every event remains open to all tiers. Principle stated on the homepage: "Attending is always free. Seeing the room isn't."
According to the homepage, memberships can be cancelled at any time, and members keep their badges, credits and attendance history.

=== SECTION: Hosting an Event - Process ===
SOURCE: https://www.techxfluence.com/host
Organizations, startups, colleges and individuals can host events on TechXfluence through the "Host an event" page. The hosting process has five steps:
1. Event Submission - enter event details such as date, venue, category and format.
2. Review and Approval - the team checks feasibility and confirms the slot.
3. Event Goes Live - the listing is published and opens for registration.
4. Promotion and Support - TechXfluence promotes the event across its channels and supports the organizer.
5. Manage Event - organizers track registrations, attendees, check-ins and analytics.

=== SECTION: Hosting an Event - Requirements and Features ===
SOURCE: https://www.techxfluence.com/host
Information needed to submit an event: event title; category (Meetup, Workshop, Webinar, Hackathon, Conference, Networking or Product Launch); date, start and end time; city and venue; ticketing type (free entry or paid tickets); capacity; organizer email and phone; event description; optional tags; and agreement to the Event Organizer Agreement and Terms and Conditions.
Platform features for organizers: registration and ticketing (sign-ups, payments, QR check-in); promotion to members through email, Discord and social media; access to a partner network for venue, prizes and budget; live analytics dashboards for registrations, attendance and feedback.
The hosting page does not list any fees or pricing, and has no FAQ section.
